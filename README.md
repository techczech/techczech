# Dominik Lukeš

I'm Dominik Lukeš, an AI consultant at the University of Oxford (Oxford e-Research Centre, AI Competency Centre). I work as a researcher, educator, writer and translator across AI, cognitive linguistics and educational technology, with long-standing work on metaphor, readability, dyslexia and accessibility. I'm a native Czech speaker and live in the UK. I build the tools I use, with AI coding agents.

## Apps

- [WriteFlex](https://writeflex.app) ([repo](https://github.com/techczech/writeflex-app)) is a Markdown writing app for long pieces, Folios and focused reading. Its AI agent proposes changes and you accept them.
- [TalkWeaver](https://talkweaver.app) ([repo](https://github.com/techczech/talkweaver-app)) is an outline-first desktop app for writing talks and presenting them as self-contained HTML decks, on macOS and Windows.
- [SlideWell](https://slidewell.app) ([repo](https://github.com/techczech/slidewell)) is a desktop archive of slides and images, and the companion to TalkWeaver.
- [Highlight Scout](https://highlightscout.app) ([repo](https://github.com/techczech/highlight-scout)) searches years of reading highlights from Readwise, Zotero, Kindle and CSV/JSON files in one local index, by keyword or by meaning. It is free and open source.
- [Dev Traffic Control](https://techczech.github.io/dev-traffic-control/) ([repo](https://github.com/techczech/dev-traffic-control)) is a Mac app for the requests coding agents send you: checks, design reviews and answers.
- [ReadabilityScout](https://github.com/techczech/readabilityscout) is a Mac app that analyses the readability of a text file: eight formulas, a grade consensus, sentence rhythm and word-level annotations.

## Agent skills

They cover research (paper review, PDF to Markdown, fact-checking, Zotero, and corpus linguistics with the Czech National Corpus and Sketch Engine), writing and readability, local speech and image models on Apple Silicon, and design and development workflows.
